Why bilingual

English for the test

Problems, vocabulary and written answers stay in English, because that's the language of the AMC and the AP exam.

Mandarin when it helps

When your child gets stuck, the explanation switches to Chinese, so the idea lands first and the English follows.

Notes for parents

A short written note after every class, in English or Chinese: what we covered, where the mistakes were, what to practice next.
